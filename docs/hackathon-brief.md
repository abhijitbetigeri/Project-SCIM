# Open World Hackathon — brief (extracted from the participant handbook)

**Build the future of physical AI through believable worlds, data-minded social environments, and
original experiments.**

- **Date / place:** Saturday **25 Jul 2026**, San Francisco, 3001 19th St
- **Build window:** 10:00–18:30 · **submissions close 18:30 PT (hard deadline)**
- **Prizes:** up to $10,000 worth · **Powered by VLGE** (hosts: Evelyn Mora, Roland Graser, Cesca Centini)
- **Event page:** https://luma.com/ttc3tp9d

## Schedule
09:00 doors · 10:00 kickoff · 13:00 lunch · 17:00 mentor check-ins · **18:30 submissions close** ·
19:45 judging + prizes · 20:00 close.

## The three tracks
1. **Open Narrative (Story + Challenge)** — believable real-world scenario/puzzle in VLGE (escape
   rooms, navigation, training scenarios; Gaussian splats or templates). *Success: a stranger enters,
   understands the goal, finishes.*
2. **VLGE Together (Social + Behavior)** — social/multiplayer world (Unity preferred, VLGE, or another
   engine) where **interaction produces useful data for robotics, physical AI, or behavior modeling**.
   Identify the robotics/physical-AI use case + the valuable behaviors/telemetry; design for future
   **consented** data (logging optional). *Success: purposeful social interaction with a credible
   future data-collection use.* ← **our track.**
3. **Free Track (Open Experimentation)** — original use of VLGE tools/data/features; state a hypothesis
   and show what the prototype proves.

## Definition of done
A judge can open it, understand it, and experience the core loop. Runs **without builder
intervention**; clear start/objective/end state; shared world/build link + video demo submitted;
external assets and AI tools disclosed.

## Judging (100 pts)
- Experience + Usability — 25
- Technical Execution — 25
- Track Fit + Impact — 20
- Originality — 20
- Demo + Reproducibility — 10

**Track-2 lens:** social interaction + usefulness and feasibility of the data-collection concept.
**Tie-breakers:** working live build · stronger core interaction · clearer provenance · greater
readiness to share. **No extra credit for unused features** — spend the last hour on reliability,
onboarding, and the demo.

## Submission (Google Form, one per team, by 18:30 PT)
Form: https://forms.gle/AgyhxvcXJGFNj9PC8
Required fields: team + participant names · project title + track + engine · one-sentence pitch ·
judge-accessible world/build link · 60–90s backup demo video · 3 strongest screenshots ·
setup/controls/expected outcome · **Track 2: future data-collection plan** · external
assets/datasets/AI tools used · repo/notes + known device requirements.
Default demo format: 3 min demo + 2 min Q&A (lead with the playable experience: problem → interaction
→ outcome).

## Rules & safety (highlights)
Solo or team; submit once. Judging covers work built/meaningfully integrated during the hackathon.
Templates/assets/datasets/AI tools allowed when **licensed and disclosed**. Do **not** capture private
spaces, identifiable people, or behavioral data without permission; if you collect data, address
consent, provenance, and removal of personal identifiers. Organizer decisions are final.
